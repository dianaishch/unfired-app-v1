Card-fill widget kit
One structure for every gap. The card asks, the chat fills it.
Structure
1
AI question
A guess to confirm, from the user’s history.
2
Answer tags or field card
Choice only: answer tags under the question, the guess highlighted, tap = answer. Anything else: a field card with Skip / Save below it.
3
Answer
User bubble + receipt with Undo.
4
Next gap
AI bubble with the next missing field. One at a time.
Rules
One field per widget. Guess first, choice second, typing last. Save writes to the card at once; Undo always. No scores or percentages.
